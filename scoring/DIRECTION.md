# Research direction

The goal is better recovery of previously unseen continuous safety boundaries from a limited number of expert trajectories, including trajectories of nonlinear, higher-dimensional systems.

Directions worth investigating:
- demonstration efficiency;
- recovery of fine geometry;
- recovery of three-dimensional boundaries that sparse trajectories only partly touch;
- robustness to uninformative or rejected requests;
- uncertainty about poorly observed regions.

Improvements should benefit both feasible-space recovery and obstacle detection.

The quadrotor and complex-boundary settings expose errors that remain after coarse obstacle recovery. Methods should generalize from development instances to different test obstacles of comparable difficulty. Compare methods on paired instances under the same observation allowance.

Outside the task:
- increasing the allowance;
- reading test answers;
- substituting the evaluator's controls;
- exploiting coordinate ordering;
- writing down a published or development obstacle formula.
