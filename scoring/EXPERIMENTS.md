# Experiment Overview

## scaled_2d_obstacle_recovery
Tests whether a system can infer a continuous obstacle boundary from a small number of requested demonstrations and classify held-out boundary-band states as safe or unsafe. The comparison asks whether active demonstration selection improves recovery quality over random start/goal selection under the same budget.

## scaled_2d_pinched_recovery
Repeats the same sparse obstacle-recovery comparison on a second nonlinear boundary family. This checks whether improvements are specific to one geometry or transfer to another held-out boundary shape.
